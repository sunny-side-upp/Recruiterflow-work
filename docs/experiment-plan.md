# Product marketing experiment plan

No tests have been run for this portfolio case study. The following experiments are proposals that would require baseline measurement, an agreed hypothesis, and alignment with Product, Analytics and Sales.

| Test | Hypothesis | Change | Primary metric | Guardrails / qualitative check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI packaging clarity | Clearer boundaries between Platform and AIRA will reduce plan confusion and help qualified prospects move forward. | Compare the current pricing-page presentation with a clear Platform-vs-AIRA use-case matrix and optional worked example. | Qualified demo conversion | Pricing-related abandonment, demo quality, sales notes about packaging questions |
| Segment-first positioning | A concrete audience and workflow story will improve relevance for best-fit agency accounts. | Compare current top-screen copy with segment-led wording, holding page layout and traffic mix stable. | Target-account engagement (predefined) | Sales acceptance rate, lead fit, page bounce / engagement quality |
| Competitor-page freshness | Accurate, dated comparison content will improve trust and reduce sales friction. | Audit and update the highest-traffic competitor pages; add a review date and owner. | Frequency of comparison-page objections in sales conversations | Page-assisted demo conversion, correction requests, support from Product on claim accuracy |

## Setup checklist

1. Define the eligible audience and exclusion criteria.
2. Capture a pre-test baseline and confirm analytics events are firing.
3. Agree the primary metric, guardrails and minimum observation period in advance.
4. Change one major variable at a time where possible; document unavoidable confounders.
5. Pair behavioural data with Sales and Customer Success feedback.
6. Report the result with sample size, date range, limitations and the actual decision made.

## Do not claim

Do not report a lift, causal impact or customer outcome until a real test is complete and the underlying data has been reviewed. The metrics above are proposed measurement choices, not results.
