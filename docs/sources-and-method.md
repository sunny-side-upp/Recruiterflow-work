## Confidence and handling rules

- **Higher confidence:** broad public product positioning when stated on the vendor's current first-party pages.
- **Medium confidence:** qualitative positioning synthesis from several public pages and review summaries.
- **Lower confidence / recheck before use:** exact competitor prices, ratings, review counts, counts of review themes, and any claim based on third-party pricing roundups.
- **Interpretive conclusions:** all three “messaging tension” findings and the recommended copy directions are analyst judgements to be validated, not established facts about conversion or sales performance.

## Limitations

- Desk research only; no private interviews, customer data, win/loss analysis or internal usage analytics were available.
- No product has been purchased or tested hands-on for this project.
- No proposed A/B test has been executed.
- No claim in this case study should be treated as a current price quote or procurement recommendation without re-verification.
- This is an independent portfolio exercise, not commissioned by or affiliated with Recruiterflow.
