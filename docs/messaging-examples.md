# Proposed messaging directions

These examples are independent copy concepts, not approved Recruiterflow messaging. They should be reviewed against the current product, brand voice, legal requirements, and customer evidence before any use.

## Direction A: make the audience and workflow concrete

**Before (public positioning paraphrase):** “AI-native intelligence with enterprise-grade depth.”

**Proposed direction:** “One recruiting workspace for candidate sourcing, client relationships and outreach — built for lean agency teams.”

**Why test it:** It names the workflow and audience before asking the buyer to interpret abstract category language. This should only be used if customer and product evidence support the segment claim.

## Direction B: explain packaging, not just the AI promise

**Proposed direction:** “Know what’s included: compare the core recruiting workflow with the AI-agent capabilities available through AIRA.”

**Why test it:** It moves the buyer toward a clear comparison and does not invent a price or claim all AI capabilities are included in the standard plan.

## Direction C: turn a generic time-saving claim into a demoable story

**Proposed direction:** “See how a recruiting team moves from sourcing to outreach to client follow-up in one workflow.”

**Why test it:** It gives Product Marketing and Sales a product walkthrough structure. A customer outcome should be added only after it is verified and approved.

## Content asset ideas

- A plan-level comparison card that shows what lives in Platform versus AIRA.
- A short feature explainer that follows one realistic recruiter workflow from sourcing to outreach and follow-up.
- A customer story built around a measurable outcome: baseline, workflow change, time-to-result and caveats.
- A sales battlecard with verified “where we differ” statements, common objections and a visible review date.
