# Sales enablement battlecard: Recruiterflow vs. the alternatives

**Audience:** Sales / Account Executives  
**Use:** Discovery and objection handling, not as an unverified feature or pricing script.  
**Research date:** 26 October 2026 — re-check claims before use.

## Start with discovery, not a feature fight

1. Which workflows are most important today: sourcing, candidate relationships, client CRM, outreach, placements or billing?
2. Which recruiting desk are you supporting (permanent, executive search, temporary staffing or a mix)?
3. How do you currently source and reach candidates, and which integrations are must-haves?
4. Is your team comparing standard workflow capabilities, AI agents, or both?
5. Where does the current process create the most manual work or handoffs?

## The short talk track

> Recruiterflow brings recruiting workflows across ATS, CRM, sourcing and outreach together, with additional AI-agent capabilities available through AIRA. The right fit depends on the desk you support, which workflows you need to connect, and which capabilities your team wants included in its plan. Let's map those needs before comparing feature lists.

Use only after confirming that the described workflows match the current product and the prospect's use case.

## Competitive conversation guide

| When the prospect mentions… | Explore… | Talk track direction | Avoid saying without current proof… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bullhorn** | Staffing complexity, billing, required integrations, operating model | Compare the workflow the prospect needs and the total effort to support it; show only relevant integrations and capabilities. | “We're always cheaper / simpler” or “we replace every staffing system.” |
| **Loxo** | Sourcing needs, AI search/agents, outreach and client-side CRM | Walk through which sourcing and AI features are included in the current plan being evaluated. | “Our AI is better” or a plan comparison that has not been rechecked. |
| **Crelate** | Client portal, business development, CRM/ATS needs, AI co-pilot | Compare the prospect's required workflows using current plan details and an agreed checklist. | Repeating older comparison copy that labels its AI or target segment inaccurately. |
| **Recruit CRM** | Ease of adoption, workflow breadth, credit-based usage, automation volume | Estimate the prospect's likely usage and compare the complete workflow and cost model fairly. | “Unlimited” or a total-cost claim without checking the latest credit / plan rules. |

## Objection-handling prompts

**“Why should we switch?”** Ask what currently fails, how often it happens, who is affected and what evidence would make a switch worthwhile. Demonstrate only the workflow that addresses the pain.

**“Does the AI come with the base plan?”** Use the current approved plan matrix. State exactly what is included in Platform and what is available via AIRA; confirm commercial details before quoting.

**“How do you compare on integrations?”** Confirm the specific integration, direction of data flow, plan limitations and any implementation dependency. Do not infer parity from a long integration list.

**“Which platform is cheaper?”** Compare the same number of seats, billing period, required modules, credits and implementation assumptions. Treat third-party estimates as directional, not quotes.

## Follow-up asset checklist

- One current plan-level comparison for the competitor discussed.
- A product walkthrough matched to the prospect's workflow.
- One customer story or measurable outcome that is approved for external use.
- A note of the objection, competitor, desk type and next step for win/loss learning.

## Claim hygiene

Before using this battlecard externally, re-verify plan names, price, AI availability, integrations, sourcing claims and competitor pages. Use approved product documentation for feature truth. This independent sample is a portfolio mockup, not approved Recruiterflow sales collateral.
